🗺️ 课程地图Lesson Map

一共 12 关，每周一关。每一关都有一个新的 Python 魔法、一个带回家的作品，还会用到前面学过的东西。🔒 还没开放的关卡，老师放好教材后就会亮起来。

12 levels, one each week. Every level has a new piece of Python magic, something to take home, and uses what you learned before. 🔒 Locked levels light up when the teacher adds the lesson.

🧭 我们的「放手」路线🧭 How we'll learn 第 1–4 关：老师做，我看（跟着做、改数字）→ 第 5–8 关：一起做（我写大部分，老师给提示）→ 第 9–12 关：我做，老师看（自己规划、自己写、自己找 bug，最后把作品发布到网上） Levels 1–4: teacher shows, I follow (copy and change numbers) → Levels 5–8: we do it together (I write most, teacher gives hints) → Levels 9–12: I do it, teacher watches (I plan, write, debug and finally publish my project online)
