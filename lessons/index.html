🗺️ 课程地图Lesson Map

一共 12 关，每周解锁一关。🔒 还没开放的关卡，老师放好教材后就会亮起来——到时候可以先来预习！

There are 12 levels — one unlocks each week. 🔒 Locked levels light up when the teacher adds the lesson, so you can preview it before class!

🧭 我们的「放手」路线🧭 How we'll learn 第 1–4 关：老师做，我看（跟着做、改数字）→ 第 5–8 关：一起做（我写大部分，老师给提示）→ 第 9–12 关：我做，老师看（自己规划、自己写、自己找 bug） Levels 1–4: teacher shows, I follow (copy and change numbers) → Levels 5–8: we do it together (I write most, teacher gives hints) → Levels 9–12: I do it, teacher watches (I plan, write and debug)
