# 🤖 <span class="zh">AI 伙伴守则</span><span class="en">AI Buddy Rules</span>

<p><span class="zh">AI 是很厉害的<b>学习伙伴</b>——但它<b>不是代写机器</b>。下面的守则，我们每节课都会用。</span><span class="en">AI is an amazing <b>learning buddy</b> — but it's <b>not a homework machine</b>. We use these rules every lesson.</span></p>

## 🧑‍✈️ <span class="zh">第 0 条：我是队长</span><span class="en">Rule 0: I'm the captain</span>

<div class="box box-think"><span class="zh">我决定做什么，AI 帮我做；<b>做得对不对，我来检查</b>。作品是我的，所以我要<b>看懂</b>它。</span><span class="en">I decide what to make and AI helps; <b>I check if it's right</b>. It's my work, so I need to <b>understand</b> it.</span></div>

## 📜 <span class="zh">三条守则</span><span class="en">Three rules</span>

<div class="levels">
  <div class="level"><div class="medal">💡</div><div><h3><span class="zh">① 学习时，要提示，不要答案</span><span class="en">① Ask for hints, not answers</span></h3><p><span class="zh">自己想出来的，才真正学会了。卡住的时候，跟 AI 说：「<b>给我一个提示，不要直接给答案</b>」。</span><span class="en">You only really learn what you figure out yourself. When stuck, tell AI: "<b>Give me a hint, not the answer</b>."</span></p></div></div>
  <div class="level"><div class="medal">🔍</div><div><h3><span class="zh">② AI 说的话要检验</span><span class="en">② Check what AI says</span></h3><p><span class="zh">AI 会犯错，有时候还会<b>编造</b>听起来很像真的东西。代码要<b>运行了才算数</b>；知识可以问老师、查资料对一对。</span><span class="en">AI makes mistakes and sometimes <b>makes things up</b> that sound true. Code only counts <b>when it runs</b>; check facts with your teacher or a book.</span></p></div></div>
  <div class="level"><div class="medal">🔒</div><div><h3><span class="zh">③ 不告诉 AI 隐私</span><span class="en">③ Keep private things private</span></h3><p><span class="zh">真名全名、地址、学校、电话、密码、照片——<b>都不发给 AI</b>。</span><span class="en">Full real name, address, school, phone number, passwords, photos — <b>never send them to AI</b>.</span></p></div></div>
</div>

## 🛟 <span class="zh">卡住了怎么办？四步法</span><span class="en">Stuck? The 4 steps</span>

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong><span class="zh">读报错</span><span class="en">Read the error</span></strong><br><span class="zh">是第几行？画板的红框会告诉你可能的原因。</span><span class="en">Which line? The red box in the playground tells you what might be wrong.</span></li>
  <li><strong><span class="zh">翻笔记本</span><span class="en">Check your notebook</span></strong><br><span class="zh">以前遇到过一样的问题吗？看看 <a href="{{ '/notebook/' | relative_url }}">📓 工程师笔记本</a>。</span><span class="en">Have you seen this before? Look in your <a href="{{ '/notebook/' | relative_url }}">📓 Engineer's Notebook</a>.</span></li>
  <li><strong><span class="zh">问 AI 要提示</span><span class="en">Ask AI for a hint</span></strong><br><span class="zh">用下面的模板问，<b>只要提示</b>。</span><span class="en">Use the template below and ask for <b>a hint only</b>.</span></li>
  <li><strong><span class="zh">再问老师</span><span class="en">Ask your teacher</span></strong><br><span class="zh">前三步都试过了还不行？太好了，说明这是个值得一起研究的问题！</span><span class="en">Tried all three? Great — that means it's a problem worth solving together!</span></li>
</ol>

## 💬 <span class="zh">问 AI 的模板</span><span class="en">Templates for asking AI</span>

<div class="card">
<p><b><span class="zh">🆘 卡住了：</span><span class="en">🆘 When I'm stuck:</span></b></p>
<pre class="zh" style="white-space:pre-wrap">我在做 ______，我想让它 ______，
但是它 ______（报错了 / 画出来不对）。
请给我一个提示，不要直接给答案。</pre>
<pre class="en" style="white-space:pre-wrap">I'm making ______. I want it to ______,
but it ______ (shows an error / draws the wrong thing).
Please give me a hint, not the answer.</pre>

<p><b><span class="zh">❓ 看不懂代码：</span><span class="en">❓ When I don't understand code:</span></b></p>
<pre class="zh" style="white-space:pre-wrap">第 ___ 行的 ______ 是什么意思？
请用小学生能听懂的话解释，再举一个小例子。</pre>
<pre class="en" style="white-space:pre-wrap">What does ______ on line ___ mean?
Please explain it in simple words and give a small example.</pre>
</div>
