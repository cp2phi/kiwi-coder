# 🛬 <span class="zh">火箭着陆</span><span class="en">Rocket Lander</span>

<p class="zh">SpaceX 猎鹰 9 号的一级助推器会自己飞回来，落在海上的无人船或者着陆场上，下次再用。这个游戏里你不按键盘——你写<b>自动驾驶程序</b>：每一拍读传感器，决定点火还是不点火。先学会物理规则，再写控制规则，最后分析试飞数据。高度、速度、燃料<b>每次都随机</b>，好的规则要对所有情况都管用！</p>
<p class="en">SpaceX's Falcon 9 first-stage boosters fly back by themselves and land on a drone ship at sea or a landing pad, so they can fly again. In this game you don't press keys — you write the <b>autopilot</b>: every tick it reads the sensors and decides whether to fire the engine. First learn the physics, then write control rules, then analyse test-flight data. Height, speed and fuel are <b>random every run</b>, so a good rule has to work in every case!</p>

<div class="box box-tip"><span class="box-title"><span class="zh">📐 游戏里的物理（简化版）</span><span class="en">📐 The game's physics (simplified)</span></span>
<span class="zh">每一拍：① 重力让速度 <b>+1</b>；② 点火的话速度 <b>−3</b>，燃料 −1；③ 高度减去速度。速度是<b>往下</b>的速度（负数 = 往上飞）。落地时速度 <b>4 或更小</b>才算安全（SAFE）。这些数字是游戏单位，不是真火箭的数字。</span>
<span class="en">Every tick: ① gravity adds <b>+1</b> to the speed; ② if the engine fires, speed <b>−3</b> and fuel −1; ③ height goes down by the speed. Speed means <b>downward</b> speed (negative = going up). Touching down at speed <b>4 or less</b> is safe (SAFE). These are game units, not real rocket numbers.</span></div>
