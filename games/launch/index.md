# 🚀 <span class="zh">发射控制</span><span class="en">Launch Control</span>

<p class="zh">你是发射控制中心的程序员！倒计时、看天气、算燃料、点名问每个岗位「行不行」——全都要写成程序。注意：<b>每次点运行，数字都会变</b>（看输出最上面的 📡 那几行），后几关还会用<b>隐藏测试</b>把你的函数试十几次。所以背答案、把数字写死都没用，你得写出<b>对所有情况都管用的规则</b>。</p>
<p class="en">You're the programmer at launch control! Countdowns, weather checks, fuel maths, polling every station "go or no go?" — it all has to be code. Watch out: <b>the numbers change every time you click Run</b> (see the 📡 lines at the top of the output), and later levels run your functions through a dozen <b>hidden tests</b>. Memorising answers or typing numbers in won't work — you need <b>rules that work for every case</b>.</p>
